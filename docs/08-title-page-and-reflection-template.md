## Introduction (sample — edit for your group)

The **International Bookstore** is a small retail business that sells books, school supplies, and related items. Manual computation of prices, discounts, and taxes at the checkout counter is slow and error-prone. Paper receipts are difficult to organize, and tracking daily sales requires separate manual records.

The **International Bookstore Sales & Receipt System** was developed to automate point-of-sale operations on a Windows desktop. The system stores product and transaction data in **SQL Server LocalDB**, supports **administrator and cashier roles**, and provides **receipt printing**, **transaction history**, and **daily sales reports**. This project demonstrates database integration, CRUD operations, and user interface design using **VB.NET Windows Forms**.
